PCT CHANGE warning

In both Munis exports, the displayed percentage generally reflects the request relative to the revised budget, despite appearing beside recommendation/approval columns. Example: FY2026 JAIL EXPAN shows 799.7%, which is the $7.24M request versus $804.7K revised—not the $5.74M approved amount. Readers should calculate their own approved/recommended change.
